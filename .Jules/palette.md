## 2025-07-15 - Centered UI and Immediate Feedback
**Learning:** Incorporating a scoring system provides an immediate feedback loop that enhances player engagement and progression. Using `text.get_rect(center=(x, y))` in Pygame ensures UI elements like 'Game Over' and final scores are consistently centered, improving visual balance and professionalism.
**Action:** Always include score tracking and centered results screens in interactive experiences to provide clear closure and feedback to the user.
