## 2025-05-18 - HUD Score and Game Over Feedback Loop
**Learning:** In fast-paced arcade games, players require immediate visual feedback on their performance during gameplay (HUD score) and a clear, centered summary upon ending (game over with final score) to establish an intuitive progression loop without screen clutter.
**Action:** Always provide persistent score tracking in the viewport and properly centered status text using rect centering rather than hardcoded coordinate offsets.
